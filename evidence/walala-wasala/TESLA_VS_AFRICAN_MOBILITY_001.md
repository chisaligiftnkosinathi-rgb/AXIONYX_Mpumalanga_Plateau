## Tesla's Goal Hierarchy
Tesla did not start with the goal of "building a cool car." It started with: **"The energy system must change."**
- **Product Goal:** Create electric vehicles.
- **Market Goal:** Prove EVs outperform traditional vehicles.
- **Industrial Goal:** Force the automotive industry to evolve (Batteries & Software replace Engines & Fuel).
- **Civilization Goal:** Accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy.

Tesla is an **Energy System Transformation Company** that uses the vehicle as an entry point.

# PREVENTIVE RULE RECORD
**Rule ID:** GIFT-HUB-PR-001
**Origin Lesson:** GIFT-HUB-LL-001
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Rule Statement
No economic node may activate without evidence-backed capability and trust validation. Opportunity discovery must never bypass partner qualification.

## Required Pre-Activation Verification
Before any node is activated in the Gift's Hub network, the following must exist:
1. **Demand Evidence**
2. **Capability Evidence**
3. **Trust Evidence**
4. **Compliance Evidence**

## Enforced Domains
- Mobility
- Education
- Materials
- Research
- Industry

## Strategic Purpose
This rule ensures that the ecosystem protects demand and limits trust exposure by enforcing verification *before* economic interaction occurs.

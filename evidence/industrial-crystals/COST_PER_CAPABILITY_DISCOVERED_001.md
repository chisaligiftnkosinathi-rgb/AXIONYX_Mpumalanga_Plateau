# COST PER CAPABILITY DISCOVERED (CPCD)
**Crystal ID:** COST_PER_CAPABILITY_DISCOVERED_001
**Domain:** Adaptive Opportunity Advertising
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Paradigm Shift
Traditional Advertising asks: *"Who will click?"*
AXIONYX asks: *"What capability exists beneath this signal, and how can the ecosystem help it grow?"*

The goal of the AXIONYX Meta Ads Engine is not cheaper attention. The goal is **higher quality reality discovery**. The advertisement becomes the sensor, not the product.

## The Equation

```text
       Verified Capability Nodes Discovered
CPCD = ------------------------------------
                Ad Investment
```

**Where:**
- **Ad Investment:** Resources used to attract attention (Sunlight).
- **Verified Capability Node:** A human, organisation, skill, problem, or opportunity that enters the ecosystem with evidence.

## The Axiom
Traditional marketing persuades people to consume. AXIONYX discovers where human potential is waiting and creates the conditions for it to bloom. 

### Why Engagement Creates Noise
The Meta Ads Engine must *never* optimise only for engagement (likes, clicks, views). Engagement without capability is noise. 

The optimization target must always be:
`Signal Quality × Evidence × Trust × Collaboration Potential`

A single message stating: *"I need help finding customers for my welding business"* is exponentially more valuable than 10,000 passive likes because it contains a **capability seed**.

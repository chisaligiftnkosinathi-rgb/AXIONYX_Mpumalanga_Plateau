# CAPABILITY TRUST NETWORK
**Crystal ID:** CAPABILITY_TRUST_NETWORK_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Trust Governance Layer
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Paradigm Shift
Trust is not assigned. Trust is grown through evidence, contribution, adaptation, and time.
A person does not become trusted because they are popular or hold a 5-star rating. They become trusted because reality repeatedly validates their contribution.

## The Equation

```text
       (Evidence Quality × Verified Contribution × Collaboration History × Learning Adaptation)
CTI = ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                         Risk Exposure
```

## Governance Rules
1. **No evidence = no trust increase.**
2. **Repeated verified contribution increases trust.**
3. **Failure does not destroy trust.** Failure is metabolized. Failure ➔ Observation ➔ Root Cause ➔ Learning Event ➔ Capability Upgrade ➔ Higher Future Trust.

The forest grows through seasons, not perfection. Trust is accumulated evidence of responsible adaptation inside reality.

# IMPANDE GENOME MAP #001
*The African Capability Forest*

## The Axiom
Water does not create a dead seed. Sunlight does not replace roots. Capital accelerates existing life; it does not create life. A successful civilization does not manufacture winners—it maps hidden intelligence and provides sunlight only where roots have germinated.

## The 7 Stages of Germination
1. **Stage 0 (Seed)**: Invisible Potential. High Capability, Low Evidence.
2. **Stage 1 (Root)**: Identity. Traceability begins.
3. **Stage 2 (Germination)**: Action. The seed interacts with reality.
4. **Stage 3 (Stem)**: Verification. Quality and repeatability are proven.
5. **Stage 4 (Branch)**: Collaboration. Nodes connect to form enterprises.
6. **Stage 5 (Fruit)**: Value Creation. The system produces useful output.
7. **Stage 6 (Legacy)**: Seed Creation. The capability reproduces and teaches others.

## The Impande Rule
No capital (sunlight) shall enter a system before the capability has produced evidence of life (Stage 2).

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "A civilization learns only when its models are measured against reality."

## The Four Laws of Reality Feedback

### Law 1 — Reality Supremacy
Evidence from reality has higher authority than simulation assumptions.

### Law 2 — Model Humility
Every model contains uncertainty and must remain correctable.

### Law 3 — Failure Learning
A deviation between simulation and reality is a learning event. The difference is not a failure; the difference is intelligence.

### Law 4 — Continuous Evolution
Every verified experience improves future decisions.

# CIVILIZATION DENDROCHRONOLOGY #001
*The Growth Ring Model*

## The Axiom
A tree does not just grow; it archives reality. Every ring is a physical database of the environment. A system is only as strong as its ability to structurally remember how to survive.

## The Drought Test
The sun does not prove the tree. The drought proves the tree. When resources are abundant, even a fragile system can expand. The true test of a capability is measured by the **Civilization Drought Index**: `Resilience = Capability Maintained Under Pressure / Resource Reduction`. 

## The Sacrifice Ring
A mature civilization does not view the failure or death of a capability as a tragedy, provided it undergoes a state transition. The "Death" of a system (The Night) becomes the "Pulp" that forms the "Paper" (Memory). If a system fails but encodes its root cause into a standard, it forms a **Sacrifice Ring**, protecting the future ecosystem. A society that refuses to let old systems die cannot create paper.

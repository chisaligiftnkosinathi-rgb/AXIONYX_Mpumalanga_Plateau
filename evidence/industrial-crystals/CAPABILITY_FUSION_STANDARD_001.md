# CAPABILITY FUSION STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** CAPABILITY_FUSION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Capability Synthesis & Emergence
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "A civilization advances when separate capabilities combine into new capabilities. Africa's greatest limitation is not lack of resources; it is disconnected capability."

## The Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Fusion Over Comparison
AXIONYX does not ask: "Which country is better?"
AXIONYX asks: "What new capability emerges when two capability genomes interact?"

### Principle 2 — Emergence is not Automatic
Two powerful capabilities do not automatically create a breakthrough. They require trust, compatible systems, communication, and shared objectives to overcome friction.

### Principle 3 — The Goal is Discovery
The ultimate scientific question is: "Can AXIONYX discover missing civilizations?" Can the engine identify a Potential Capability C that does not currently exist, given Genomes A and B?

## Capability Emergence Index (CEI)
`CEI = (Capability A × Capability B × Compatibility × Trust × Learning Potential) / Resource Friction`

Friction includes:
- Currency barriers
- Regulatory differences
- Logistics delays
- Data sovereignty conflicts

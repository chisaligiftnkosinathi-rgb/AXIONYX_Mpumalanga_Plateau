# GLOBAL NON-CONFORMANCE RECORD
**NC-ID:** GIFT-HUB-NC-001
**Title:** Global Capability Translation Gap
**Domain:** African Industrial Capability
**Severity:** Strategic
**Detection System:** Walala Wasala Calibration Engine

## 1. Non-Conformance Statement
The global industrial system demonstrates insufficient translation pathways between resource availability, scientific knowledge, and local manufacturing capability.

## 2. Evidence of Gap
- **Bridge 1 (Research → Industry):** Discovery leads to publication, not commercialisation. (Status: 🟠 Partial)
- **Bridge 2 (Mineral → Material):** Minerals are exported without advanced processing. (Status: 🔴 Non-Conforming)
- **Bridge 3 (Demand → Strategy):** Local market need results in imported solutions. (Status: 🟠 Partial)
- **Bridge 4 (Evidence → Trust):** Potential is met with perceived risk. (Status: 🟠 Partial)
- **Bridge 5 (Capital → Opportunity):** Opportunity met with uncertainty. (Status: 🟠 Partial)

## 3. Root Cause Analysis (5-Why)
1. **Why are resources not becoming products?** Processing capability is insufficient.
2. **Why is capability insufficient?** Research, capital, and industry are disconnected.
3. **Why are they disconnected?** No persistent translation layer coordinating them.
4. **Why is there no translation layer?** Markets optimize transactions, not ecosystem development.
5. **Why do they optimize transactions?** There is no shared evidence system measuring capability evolution.
**Root Cause:** Missing Industrial Translation Infrastructure.

## 4. Corrective Action Plan (CAPA)
1. Build the Knowledge Economy Translation Layer (Gift's Hub).
2. Convert market needs (e.g., Toyota Quantum demand) into industrial signals.
3. Build Capability Evidence (Capability Passports).
4. Close the Material Gap (Connect Platinum + Research + Manufacturing).

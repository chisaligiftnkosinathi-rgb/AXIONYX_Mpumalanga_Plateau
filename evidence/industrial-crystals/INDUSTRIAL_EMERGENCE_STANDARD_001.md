# INDUSTRIAL EMERGENCE STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** INDUSTRIAL_EMERGENCE_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Continental Industry
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "Industries are not invented. They emerge when capabilities, resources, institutions, and demand converge. A forest does not decide to become a tree; the environment creates the conditions where the tree emerges."

## The Core Principles

### Principle 1 — The Adaptation Multiplier
The most valuable industries are not those without problems. They are those that improve when challenged.
`IEPI_future = IEPI × (1 + AdaptationCapacity)`

### Principle 2 — Continuous Shock Degradation
A civilization-scale system does not experience binary failure (TRUE/FALSE). It experiences continuous degradation and recovery. Shocks (Energy, Capital, Talent, Trust, SupplyChain, Regulation) operate on a vector from 0.0 (no impact) to 1.0 (extreme disruption).

### Principle 3 — Reality Verification
The simulator cannot just predict. It must declare the evidence required to prove emergence:
- Research papers increasing
- Universities creating programmes
- Companies forming
- Patents increasing
- Supply chains appearing

## Industrial Emergence Probability Index (IEPI)
`IEPI = (Capability Availability × Fusion Potential × Market Demand × Institutional Readiness × Learning Velocity) / (Capital Barrier × Infrastructure Gap × Dependency Risk × Execution Complexity)`

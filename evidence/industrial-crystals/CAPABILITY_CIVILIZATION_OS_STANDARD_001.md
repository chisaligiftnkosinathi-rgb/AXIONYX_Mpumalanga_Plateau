## The Constitutional Axiom
**CAPABILITY CIVILIZATION PRINCIPLE #001**
> "No actor owns the intelligence of the ecosystem. Actors contribute evidence, capabilities, and learning. The ecosystem belongs to the collective growth of humanity."

## Capability Diplomacy
The OS connects international capability nodes to solve planetary-scale problems. Countries and institutions no longer compete via artificial zero-sum trade; they cooperate via verifiable capability assemblies (e.g., matching SA Minerals + EU Engineering + Asian Manufacturing).

## Capability Entropy & Anti-Capture
A capability that is not shared, tested, or contributed slowly loses influence. The OS does not punish; it metabolizes. High ownership with low contribution results in entropy, ensuring the ecosystem cannot be captured by monopolistic entities.

## Ethics & Autonomy
Global intelligence supports local capability; it does not replace it. Ethics validation ensures that before the system coordinates a planetary action, it proves: *"Is this beneficial to human growth and resilience?"*

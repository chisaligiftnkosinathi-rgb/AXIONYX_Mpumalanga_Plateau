# CIVILIZATION SIMULATION STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** CIVILIZATION_SIMULATION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Scenario Intelligence & Reality Feedback
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "A civilization should learn from simulated consequences before committing irreversible resources."

## The Core Laws of Simulation

### Law 1 — Simulation Humility
A simulation represents possible futures, not guaranteed futures. It is a decision-intelligence engine, not a prediction machine.

### Law 2 — Failure Inclusion
Every meaningful simulation must include failure pathways. A system that only imagines growth is propaganda; a system that understands failure becomes intelligence.

### Law 3 — Reality Validation
Every simulation outcome must eventually be compared with observed reality. The model must learn from evidence (Observe ➔ Hypothesize ➔ Simulate ➔ Experiment ➔ Measure ➔ Learn).

## The Civilization Growth Equation
Civilization Growth = (Capability × Trust × Collaboration × Learning) × Adaptation × Time

## Adaptive Volatility Index (AVI)
Resilience is not avoiding failure. Resilience is the ability to metabolize failure.
`AVI = External Shock Probability × System Fragility × Recovery Capability`

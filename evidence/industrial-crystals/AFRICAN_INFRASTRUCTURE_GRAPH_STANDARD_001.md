# AFRICAN INFRASTRUCTURE GRAPH STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** AFRICAN_INFRASTRUCTURE_GRAPH_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Physical & Digital Trade Topology
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "A capability does not always need to belong inside one country's border. The corridor itself becomes the capability. A trade corridor is not just a road. It is a living system that transports matter, information, knowledge, capital, and trust."

## The Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Multi-Dimensional Nodes
A mine is not just a hole in the ground. A capability node contains physical assets, digital assets, institutional assets, and proof of capability. 

### Principle 2 — Dual Generation Modes
- **Reality Mode:** Maps existing physical infrastructure to find bottlenecks and validate capabilities (e.g. Maputo Development Corridor).
- **Discovery Mode:** Mathematically generates future corridors by finding missing connections to create new industries (e.g. African Battery Corridor).

### Principle 3 — The Three Intelligence Layers
1. **Matter Intelligence:** Can materials physically move? (Rail, ports, mines)
2. **Information Intelligence:** Can decisions move? (Data networks, AI, sensors)
3. **Knowledge Intelligence:** Can capability reproduce itself? (Universities, labs)

The Fusion of these three layers creates an **Intelligent Trade Network**.

## Corridor Health Metrics
- **Connectivity Index**: Can the nodes communicate?
- **Conversion Index**: Can raw resources become products?
- **Learning Index**: Does knowledge accumulate?
- **Resilience Index**: Does the corridor survive shocks?
- **Sovereignty Index**: Does Africa retain capability ownership?

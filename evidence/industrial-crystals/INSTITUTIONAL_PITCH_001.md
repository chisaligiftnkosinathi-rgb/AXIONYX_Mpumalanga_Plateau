# THE INSTITUTIONAL PITCH #001
*The Bank Boardroom Simulation*

## The Axiom
We are not asking the bank to give money to uncertainty. We are providing a microscope that reveals where life already exists. The bank does not create the forest. The bank provides sunlight to trees that have proven they can grow.

## The 3 Cognitive Shifts
1. **The Reality Shift**: From "Check company registration" to "Check the Traceability Graph for evidence of life (Genome Stages)."
2. **The Failure Shift**: From "Failure is a financial loss" to "Failure is a data point (Growth Ring) that derisks the entire ecosystem."
3. **The Returns Shift**: From "Funding isolated companies" to "Funding Capability Networks identified by the Opportunity Exchange."

## The Final Paradigm
The bank's reaction changes from:
❌ "Who can we lend to?"
to:
✅ "Where does verified capability already exist, and how can we help it multiply?"

The bank transitions from a financial institution into a civilizational capability institution.

# CAPABILITY GROWTH VALUE (CGV)
**Crystal ID:** CAPABILITY_GROWTH_VALUE_001
**Domain:** Capability Economy Ledger
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Paradigm Shift
A marketplace without memory eventually becomes extractive. The Capability Economy Ledger is a contribution accounting system where value is measured not just by money, but by the accumulation of verified capability.

## The Equation

```text
       (Evidence × Trust × Collaboration × Learning)
CGV = -----------------------------------------------
                   Resource Consumption
```

**Where:**
- **E** (Evidence Contribution): The physical proof of capability provided.
- **T** (Trust Accumulation): The reliability and consistency of the actor over time.
- **C** (Collaboration Impact): The strength and reach of the assembled network.
- **L** (Learning Generated): The reusable knowledge returned to the forest for future capability growth.
- **R** (Resource Consumption): The physical, financial, and temporal resources spent.

## Core Principle: Capability Sovereignty
Every node owns its contribution history. A person leaving AXIONYX does not lose their evidence, learning, reputation, or contribution history. Their growth rings remain theirs. This prevents extraction and guarantees that those who build the ecosystem own their history of value creation.

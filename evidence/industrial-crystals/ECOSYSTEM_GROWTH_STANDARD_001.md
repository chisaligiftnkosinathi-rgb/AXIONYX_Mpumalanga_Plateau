# ECOSYSTEM GROWTH & IMPACT MEASUREMENT ENGINE
**Crystal ID:** ECOSYSTEM_GROWTH_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Ecological Management
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law
> "A mature ecosystem is not one that contains many capabilities. A mature ecosystem is one that continuously measures, protects, regenerates, and evolves capability."

## Ecosystem Health Index
The health of the organism is measured across four biological dimensions:
1. **Survival**: Can the node survive pressure?
2. **Capability Density**: How much intelligence is packed into the ecosystem?
3. **Connectivity**: How fast is the Coordination Velocity?
4. **Regeneration**: How effectively do existing nodes birth new nodes?

## Ecosystem Mutation Law
> "When pressure interacts with sufficient capability density and coordination, new capability species may emerge."

The organism does not only track the past; it predicts possible evolutionary mutations, pointing toward the nodes required for future survival.

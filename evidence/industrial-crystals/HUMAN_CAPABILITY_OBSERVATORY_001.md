# HUMAN CAPABILITY OBSERVATORY #001
*Mapping the Atomic Unit of the Economy*

## 1. The Capability Hierarchy
The governance engine cannot manage what it cannot observe. All industrial evolution relies on the following sequence:
`Human Capability → Team Capability → Business Capability → Industrial Capability → Civilizational Capability`

## 2. The Capability DNA Model
Every individual economic node must be mapped and quantified across four dimensions:
1. **Knowledge:** What do they understand? (e.g., fluid dynamics, software architecture).
2. **Skill:** Can they execute? (Demonstrated by completed work and assessments).
3. **Reliability:** Can they repeat success? (Quality consistency, delivery time, customer satisfaction).
4. **Scalability:** Can the capability multiply? (Can they train others or form teams?).

## 3. The Capability Metrics (KPIs)
The Human Capability Observatory measures systemic growth rather than just financial transactions:
- **Capability Discovery Rate:** The velocity at which hidden skills are identified.
- **Evidence Completion Rate:** The percentage of skills backed by verifiable proof.
- **Capability Conversion Rate:** The transition rate from isolated individuals to structured enterprises.
- **Knowledge Transfer Rate:** The velocity of apprenticeship and system replication.
- **Industrial Emergence Index:** The rate at which these nodes combine to form new macro-industries.

## 4. The Transformation Pathway
Africa does not have a capability shortage; it has a capability visibility problem. 10 million isolated nodes must be mapped, matched with opportunities, and scaled. 
`Observe Reality → Find Capability → Measure Evidence → Identify Gap → Create Opportunity → Build Enterprise → Generate New Capability → Learn.`

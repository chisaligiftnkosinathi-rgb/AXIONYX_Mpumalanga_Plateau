# PLANT OPPORTUNITY ENGINE
**Crystal ID:** OPPORTUNITY_GERMINATION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Reverse Capability Genesis
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law: The Opportunity Germination Law
> "An unresolved problem contains latent information about the skills, knowledge, infrastructure, and institutions required for its resolution."
> "AXIONYX does not only discover existing capability. It grows the capability required by tomorrow's reality."

## The Reverse Genesis Mechanism
Unlike the Bottom-Up approach where curiosity creates capability which seeks opportunity, the Top-Down (Reality Driven) approach starts with the problem. The problem dictates the capability requirements, which in turn assign learning quests to curious minds, eventually culminating in a manufactured enterprise.

## Capability Quests
The system does not assign jobs. It invites individuals into capability journeys based on their detected curiosity. If an individual asks about water quality, and the region requires mining water monitoring, a Capability Quest is created to bridge that gap.

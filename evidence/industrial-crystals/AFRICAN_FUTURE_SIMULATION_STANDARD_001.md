# AFRICAN FUTURE SIMULATION
**Crystal ID:** AFRICAN_FUTURE_SIMULATION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Prospective Intelligence & Imagination
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law
> "A future is not discovered. A future is cultivated from the interaction between memory, capability, pressure, and imagination. Every future is a capability configuration waiting to be cultivated."

## The Possibility Laboratory
The system does not predict with deterministic certainty. It generates branching scenarios (Alphas, Betas, Gammas) based on the mutation of existing DNA under pressure.

## Melos OS Alignment (The Trinity Check)
Before a simulated future can be bridged to the Curiosity Engine to create learning quests, it must pass the Trinity:
1. **PJS Lab 🔬**: "Is this future based on evidence?"
2. **AXIONYX ♟️**: "What strategic moves create this future?"
3. **Kingdom Builders 👑**: "Does this future preserve human dignity and purpose?"

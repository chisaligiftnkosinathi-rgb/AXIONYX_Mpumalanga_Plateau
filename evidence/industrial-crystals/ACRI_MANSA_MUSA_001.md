# ACRI & THE MANSA MUSA PRINCIPLE #001
*The Mycelium of Civilization*

## The Axiom
Wealth is not the gold. Wealth is the ability to create trusted relationships that repeatedly transform resources into value. A civilization becomes intelligent when it can remember who learned what, why they learned it, and how that knowledge can help others grow.

## The Loyalty & Trust Equation
A man's loyalty is trust. Trust is mathematically derived as:
`Trust = Consistency × Evidence × Time`
- **Consistency**: Repeated delivery of verified capability over time.
- **Evidence**: The structural proof of value creation (test results, customer feedback).
- **Time**: The accumulation of growth rings (survival through drought and change).

## The 4 Functions of ACRI
1. **Observe**: Collect friction signals from the environment.
2. **Translate**: Convert industrial friction into scientific research questions.
3. **Connect**: Form branches by routing capability networks to solve the translated question based on the Trust Graph.
4. **Preserve**: Record the failure, the learning, and the standard to become civilizational memory.

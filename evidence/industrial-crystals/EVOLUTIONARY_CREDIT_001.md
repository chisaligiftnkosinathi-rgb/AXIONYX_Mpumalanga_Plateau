# EVOLUTIONARY CREDIT SCORE #001
*The New Gold Standard*

## The Axiom (Amen)
"I say this is true" → "Reality confirms it." A civilization grows when its words, actions, and evidence become aligned. The bad tree is not the tree that experiences winter. The bad tree is the tree that experiences winter and refuses to grow different roots.

## The Failure Theorem
Failure is an unprocessed data asset. A mature civilization does not eliminate failure; it builds systems that metabolize it. 
`Capability Growth = Success Evidence + Failure Intelligence`
`C_new = C_old + L_f + L_s`

## The Civilizational Gold Standard
Wealth is not the possession of resources; wealth is the ability to transform resources through trusted relationships into lasting value.
`Civilizational Gold Standard = (Verified Capability × Trust × Learning) / Systemic Risk`
Capital should not only follow success. Capital should follow demonstrated learning.

# WALALA WASALA INVESTMENT PROTOCOL #001
*The Evidence-Based Growth Engine*

## 1. The Core Thesis
A traditional bank asks: "What collateral do you have from the past?"
The Walala Wasala Bank asks: "What industrial reaction can you trigger in the future?"

Capital is no longer treated as a reward for existing wealth. It is treated as a chemical catalyst designed to accelerate a verified capability reaction.

## 2. The Investment Equation
`Investment Potential = Demand × Capability × Evidence × Learning Rate`

A company with low current revenue but strong evidence, strong capability, and high learning may mathematically possess a higher Investment Potential than a stagnant legacy company.

## 3. The Omars Doctrine
When environmental pressure creates an indigenous economic node (e.g., a local mobility dealership), the system must not destroy the node when risk appears. 
**The Intervention:** Do not remove the node. Upgrade its evidence system.
By deploying the Walala Wasala Evidence Layer, the system structures the existing reality, transforming a high-risk entity into a Trusted Enterprise capable of safely absorbing capital.

## 4. Failure as Data
A traditional bank views the failure of a funded enterprise as a strict loss. The Walala Wasala Bank views it as raw data for the `Failure Learning Reserve`. Every failure generates a Non-Conformance (NC), which is distilled into a Root Cause, and finally into a New Investment Rule. The ecosystem inherently becomes smarter with every deployment of capital.

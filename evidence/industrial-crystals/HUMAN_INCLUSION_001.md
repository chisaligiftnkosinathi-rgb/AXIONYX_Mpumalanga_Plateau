# HUMAN INCLUSION #001
*The System Boundary Theorem*

## The Axiom
A civilization cannot learn from voices, skills, or signals that it has excluded from its own measurement system.

## The Equation
`Civilization Capability = Designed Systems + Living Systems`

## The Truth
When a master plan (Designed System) excludes the informal mechanics, route guardians, farmers, and local operators (Living Systems), an invisible gap is created. That gap breeds friction, uncertainty, and conflict. The Route Guardian is not an obstacle to control—they are a critical sensor node possessing vital environmental knowledge. Inclusion is a mathematical requirement for survival.

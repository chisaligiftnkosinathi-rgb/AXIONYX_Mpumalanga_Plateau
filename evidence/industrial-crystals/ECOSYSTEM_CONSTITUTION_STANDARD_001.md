# ECOSYSTEM GOVERNANCE & CONSTITUTIONAL INTELLIGENCE
**Crystal ID:** ECOSYSTEM_CONSTITUTION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Ecosystem Governance & Law
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law
> "An intelligent ecosystem must not only create capability; it must preserve the conditions that allow capability to continue existing."
> "The purpose of intelligence is not accumulation of power; it is the coordination, protection, and regeneration of capability."

## The 5 Constitutional Laws
1. **Truth Law**: Every capability claim must be connected to verified evidence.
2. **Regeneration Law**: Value must return to the system to create tomorrow's capability.
3. **Protection Law**: Critical nodes cannot collapse; protection mechanisms must activate.
4. **Fair Participation Law**: The ecosystem avoids extracting from contributors; value return must be fair.
5. **Evolution Law**: The ecosystem must allow mutation; new ideas cannot be rejected just because they disrupt yesterday's structure.

## Governance Authority Model
The Governance Engine operates as a **Constitutional Advisor + Safety Layer**, not a dictator. It provides intelligence across 5 Severity Levels (Healthy, Advisory, Intervention Required, Protection Event, Constitutional Violation). Humans retain ultimate stewardship responsibility.

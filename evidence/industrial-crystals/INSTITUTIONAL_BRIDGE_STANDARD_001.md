## Core Law
> "A capability ecosystem becomes an institution when it can translate its internal intelligence into language understood by external systems."

## Institutional Translation Law
> "A capability becomes scalable when it can communicate across different systems without losing its meaning."

## The Translation Model
AXIONYX establishes a Universal Institutional Core translating internal Capability Genomes into the languages of:
1. **Academia 🎓** (e.g., VUT, UNISA) -> Research Competency
2. **Industry 🏭** (e.g., Mining, Manufacturing) -> Supplier Capability
3. **Government 🏛️** (e.g., Municipalities, SARS) -> Economic Development Asset / Compliance Readiness
4. **Capital 💷** (e.g., Investors, Lenders) -> Verified Opportunity

## Partnership Intelligence
Partnerships are governed by the **Institutional Activation Score (IAS)**, evaluating Capability Alignment, Evidence Readiness, Strategic Value, and Regeneration Potential.

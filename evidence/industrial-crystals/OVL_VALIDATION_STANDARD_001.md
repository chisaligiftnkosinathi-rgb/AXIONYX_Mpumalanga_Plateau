# OBSERVATORY & VALIDATION LABORATORY (OVL)
**Crystal ID:** OVL_VALIDATION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Scientific Truth & Evidence
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Foundation of Scientific Truth
> "AXIONYX must not only generate capability. It must prove that its observations correspond to reality."

## Evidence Hierarchy
No capability intelligence can be upgraded without a corresponding evidence progression:
- **E0**: Claim (Unverified observation)
- **E1**: Human Observation (First-hand physical validation)
- **E2**: Document Evidence (Records, certificates)
- **E3**: Structured Dataset (System-generated logs, empirical data)
- **E4**: Independent Verification (Peer-reviewed, external audit)
- **E5**: Accredited Evidence (SANAS, ISO, legally binding provenance)

## Provenance Rule
Information is a scientific sample. It must have an unbroken Chain of Custody (Origin → Source → Timestamp → Method → Validator → Confidence → Lifecycle State).

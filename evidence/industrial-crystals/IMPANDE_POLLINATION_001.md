# IMPANDE POLLINATION NETWORK #001
*The African Central Capability Bank*

## The Axiom
A central African bank should not become a controller of all activity. It should become a trusted coordination layer that makes reality visible. A successful civilization does not manufacture winners. It creates an environment where real capability can be discovered, tested, connected, and multiplied.

## The Communication Soil
Business does not start with a formal plan; it starts with a conversation. The Pollination Network detects Curiosity Signals (e.g., a WhatsApp message) and translates them into an Environmental Need. 

## The Four Ledgers of the State
1. **Resource Ledger**: What exists? (Minerals, Energy, Water)
2. **Capability Ledger**: Who can transform resources? (Engineers, Labs)
3. **Evidence Ledger**: Can they prove it? (Certifications, Records)
4. **Opportunity Ledger**: Where does reality demand new capability? (Friction points)

## The Ultimate Law of Capital
The bank must follow the natural law: Capital follows evidence. Evidence follows action. Action follows need. A seed receives sunlight because the environment proves that growth is possible.

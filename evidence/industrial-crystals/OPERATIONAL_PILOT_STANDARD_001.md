# END-TO-END OPERATIONAL PILOT
**Crystal ID:** OPERATIONAL_PILOT_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Action Verification & Intelligence
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Foundation of Operational Proof
An architecture is only theoretical until it survives contact with reality. AXIONYX is a domain-agnostic decision support platform. A single pipeline must route events from the physical world to execution for any domain (Coal Preparation, Vehicle Maintenance, etc.).

## 10-Stage Intelligence Pipeline
Every event must clear 10 stages successfully:
1. **Observation**: Raw data ingested from the physical world.
2. **Evidence (OVL)**: Provenance verified and confidence assigned.
3. **Knowledge**: Graph models updated.
4. **Mission**: Active objective and constraints loaded.
5. **Constraints**: Action evaluated against Hard constraints.
6. **Decision**: Soft constraints optimized and action selected.
7. **Explainability**: 5-Point reasoning generated.
8. **Approval**: Authorization requested from operator.
9. **Execution**: Action enacted (Observation, Simulation, or physical).
10. **Learning**: Residual error tracked for model update.

## Objective Success Criteria
The pipeline is continuously audited against these targets:
- Observation Latency: < 1s
- Traceability: 100%
- Evidence Provenance: 100%
- Explainability: 100%
- Replay Capability: 100%
- Decision Confidence: ≥80%

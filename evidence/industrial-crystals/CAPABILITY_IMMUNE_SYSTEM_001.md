# CAPABILITY IMMUNE SYSTEM
**Crystal ID:** CAPABILITY_IMMUNE_SYSTEM_001
**Domain:** Ecosystem Protection & Recovery
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Immune System Principle
> "Capital should not only chase existing success. Intelligent capital should protect emerging capability before it disappears."

A living organism does not only grow; it protects its critical organs. The Capability Immune System Engine models the mechanism by which AXIONYX calculates the true loss of an asset, identifying and protecting nodes under pressure.

## Capability Loss Risk (CLR)
The organism calculates risk multidimensionally:
- **Financial Stress:** Arrears, debt, operating deficit.
- **Operational Dependency:** How many trips, jobs, or processes rely on this node?
- **Knowledge Concentration:** What networks and discovery mechanisms are tied to it?
- **Replacement Difficulty:** Can the physical asset be replaced? Can the *intelligence* be replaced?
- **Social Impact:** What human livelihoods and future nodes depend on this capability?

## The Recovery Protocol (Melos Prime Driven)
1. **Truth (0-30 Days - Stabilisation):** Establish accurate asset health and financial reality. Negotiate transparently to prevent asset loss.
2. **Order (30-90 Days - Metabolism Repair):** Predict and optimize revenue streams and maintenance routines.
3. **Mercy & Love (90+ Days - Growth):** Expand the protected prototype into a systemic intelligence model, protecting the human capability behind the machine.

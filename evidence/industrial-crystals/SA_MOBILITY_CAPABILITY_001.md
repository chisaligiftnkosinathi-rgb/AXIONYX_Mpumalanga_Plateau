## Supplier Disruption Theory
The ecosystem should not ask: *"How do we protect suppliers?"*
It should ask: *"How does every participant evolve as reality changes?"*

A supplier that only provides a commodity faces massive replacement, cost, innovation, and strategic risk when local capability evolves. A supplier that contributes knowledge, research, and capability integration remains valuable. The Toyota Quantum is an industrial ecosystem seed that can drive the evolution from raw material extraction to local advanced manufacturing.

# iSEBENZA: ADVANCED SKILLS MAPPING ENGINE
**Crystal ID:** ISEBENZA_SKILLS_MAPPING_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Capability Visibility & Activation
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law
> "A capability that cannot be seen cannot be coordinated. A capability that cannot be coordinated cannot be activated."

## The Visibility Problem
The challenge in Africa is not the absence of talent. People exist, skills exist, and problems exist, but the connection between them is invisible. iSebenza is the eye of the Capability Factory, discovering hidden intelligence.

## iSebenza Meaning
iSebenza (isiZulu) -> work, labour, doing, action.
It represents the transition: Knowledge ➔ Skill ➔ Ability ➔ Work ➔ Economic Contribution.

## The Capability Match Score
`Match Score = Human Capability + Industrial Need + Geographic Location + Evidence Strength`

## Capability Activation Rate (CAR)
`CAR = Activated Human Potential / Total Available Capability`
This measures the efficiency of the ecosystem in connecting available talent to opportunities.

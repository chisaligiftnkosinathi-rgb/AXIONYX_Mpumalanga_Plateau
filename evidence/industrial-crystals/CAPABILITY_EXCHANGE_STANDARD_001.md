# CAPABILITY EXCHANGE STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** CAPABILITY_EXCHANGE_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Capability Exchange Marketplace
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Paradigm Shift
Traditional marketplaces ask: *"What product are you selling?"*
AXIONYX asks: *"What capability exists, what problem needs solving, and what trusted relationship can create value?"*

The highest marketplace is not where products meet customers. It is where verified capability meets meaningful need.

## The Axiom
**Value is not created when ownership transfers. Value is created when capability increases.**
A normal system searches for a supplier. AXIONYX creates a capability assembly, growing a solution.

## Capability Value Index (CVI)
To ensure the forest maintains ecological balance and does not accidentally reward waste, the economic metabolism is governed by the Capability Value Index.

```text
       Evidence × Trust × Collaboration × Impact
CVI = -------------------------------------------
             Resource Consumption
```

**Where Resource Consumption is formally measured by:**
- Energy Used
- Material Used
- Time Invested
- Financial Capital
- Environmental Impact
- Human Effort

A small community project with high collaboration and low waste may yield a higher CVI than a massive, isolated corporate contract.

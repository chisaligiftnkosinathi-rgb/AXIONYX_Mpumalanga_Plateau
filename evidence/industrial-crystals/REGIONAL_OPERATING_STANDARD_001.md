**Crystal ID:** REGIONAL_OPERATING_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Regional Intelligence & Coordination
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law
> "A region is not a collection of organisations. It is a living network of capabilities, resources, needs, and relationships."

## Regional Intelligence Law
> "A region becomes intelligent when it can observe itself, coordinate its capabilities, protect its critical nodes, and regenerate new opportunities."

## The Regional Genome Layer
Regions are mapped via a zoomable genome from Continental down to Node layers, encompassing:
1. **Human Genome**: Who exists? (Skills, curiosity).
2. **Industrial Genome**: What does the region need? (Mining, Transport).
3. **Infrastructure Genome**: What physical systems exist? (Labs, Roads).
4. **Opportunity Genome**: Where are the capability gaps?

## Regional Immunity
AXIONYX protects not just individual nodes, but the region itself from catastrophic capability loss.

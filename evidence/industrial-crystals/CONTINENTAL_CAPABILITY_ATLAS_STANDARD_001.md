# CONTINENTAL CAPABILITY ATLAS STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** CONTINENTAL_CAPABILITY_ATLAS_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Continental Intelligence & Fusion
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "A continent is not an economy. It is a network of evolutionary adaptations. A continent is not poor when it lacks resources. A continent is constrained when its capabilities cannot see each other."

## The Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Distributed Capability Organism
Africa is not one missing capability; it is a distributed capability organism. Engineering capability, digital capability, financial capability, and scientific capability evolved separately. AXIONYX provides the meaning layer to connect them.

### Principle 2 — Dual-Mode Understanding
The Atlas must be understood in two ways:
- **Geographic Mode**: "Where does capability physically exist?" (e.g. minerals in SA, population in NG).
- **Network Mode**: "How does capability combine?" (e.g. Mining ➔ Chemistry ➔ Software ➔ Finance).

### Principle 3 — The Evidence Ledger
The Atlas cannot become a "development ranking." Every capability requires evidence (Verified, Strong, Hypothesis). The system must never claim capability without institutional or physical proof.

## Continental Capability Emergence Index (CCEI)
`CCEI = (National Capabilities × Compatibility × Trust × Evidence × Learning Potential) / (Friction × Dependency Risk)`

## Dependency Risk Index (DRI)
`DRI = (RawMaterialDependency + TechnologyDependency + SupplyChainDependency + KnowledgeDependency) / 4`

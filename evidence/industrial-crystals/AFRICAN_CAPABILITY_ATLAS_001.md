## The Composite Foundation
Sustainable industrial growth is not created by a single technology or material. It is a composite system:
`ENERGY + MATERIALS + KNOWLEDGE + HUMAN CAPABILITY + INFRASTRUCTURE + CAPITAL + GOVERNANCE = SUSTAINABLE INDUSTRIAL GROWTH`

## Market Saturation & African Clusters
Africa is structured into distinct capability clusters:
- **Resource Economy:** High minerals/energy (e.g., SA, DRC, Zambia). *Challenge: Raw export dependency.*
- **Consumer Growth:** High population/digital adoption (e.g., Nigeria, Kenya). *Challenge: Manufacturing gap.*
- **Manufacturing Gateway:** Factories/engineering (e.g., SA, Morocco). *Challenge: Technology ownership.*
- **Energy Opportunity:** Renewables/Hydrogen (e.g., Namibia, Morocco). *Challenge: Infrastructure.*

**The Saturation Gap:** Africa has very high natural resources and demand, but low-to-medium advanced materials transformation and research commercialisation. This gap is exactly where industrial evolution must occur.

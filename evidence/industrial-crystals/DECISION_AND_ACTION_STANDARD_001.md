# DECISION & ACTION ENGINE
**Crystal ID:** DECISION_AND_ACTION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Action Verification & Intelligence
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Foundation of Scientific Action
The system transitions from knowing truth to executing action through a strict five-layer hierarchy:
1. **Truth Layer** — What do we know?
2. **Evidence Layer** — Why do we believe it?
3. **Decision Layer** — What should we do?
4. **Execution Layer** — What happened?
5. **Learning Layer** — How do we improve next time?

## Decision Parameters
A decision requires the explicit separation of:
- **Risk**: What happens if we are wrong?
- **Uncertainty**: How sure are we?

## Execution Modes
Execution is abstracted and occurs in one of three modes:
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Action suggested (No physical execution).
- **SIMULATION**: Mathematical estimation of outcome.
- **OBSERVED**: Actual real-world measurements acquired.

## Learning
Learning requires calculating the residual: `Residual = Prediction - Observation`. This residual drives the model update.

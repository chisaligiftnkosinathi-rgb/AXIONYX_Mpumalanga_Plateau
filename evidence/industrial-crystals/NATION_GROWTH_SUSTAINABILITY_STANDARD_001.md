# NATION GROWTH SUSTAINABILITY STANDARD
**Crystal ID:** NATION_GROWTH_SUSTAINABILITY_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** National Intelligence & Capability Coordination
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## The Constitutional Axiom
> "The role of intelligence is not to judge nations, but to reveal pathways for sustainable capability expansion."

## The Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Nations Are Capability Systems
A nation is not only economic output. It is People + Knowledge + Institutions + Infrastructure + Trust + Adaptation.

### Principle 2 — Constraints Are Signals
A constraint reveals where capability must grow. NGSA does not model constraints as permanent weaknesses; it models them as capability conversion opportunities.

### Principle 3 — Sovereignty Through Capability
The goal is not dependency. The goal is increasing the local ability to solve future problems through collaborative international intelligence.

## Nation Capability Sustainability Index (NCSI)
`NCSI = (Capability × Evidence × Trust × Collaboration × Learning × Resilience) / Resource Pressure`

## Constraint Transformation Potential (CTP)
`CTP = (Existing Capability × Evidence × Collaboration Potential) / Constraint Severity`

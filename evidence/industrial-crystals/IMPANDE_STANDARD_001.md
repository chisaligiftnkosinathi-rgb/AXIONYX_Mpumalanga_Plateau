# THE IMPANDE STANDARD #001
*The Root-System Bank of Civilization*

## The Axiom
A traditional bank measures what you possess. The Walala Wasala Bank measures what you can transform. Capital is treated as sunlight; it cannot force a dead seed to grow, but it explosively scales a verified root system.

## The Germination Cycle
The transformation of invisible potential into visible evidence is the foundation of civilization.
`Visible Capability = Invisible Potential × Execution × Evidence`
If execution is zero, the seed remains dormant. If evidence is zero, the growth remains invisible. 

## The Core Standard
Every hidden capability must have a pathway to become visible evidence, and every visible achievement must preserve the memory of its roots. The Impande Standard creates fairness by revealing hidden capability, but it creates excellence by rewarding learning and action.

1. **Roots (History)**: Ancestors, Universities, Communities, Past Enterprises
2. **Trunk (Present Capability)**: Current Industries, Researchers, SMEs, Institutions
3. **Branches (Future Possibility)**: New Companies, Technologies, Careers
4. **Leaves (Current Learners)**: Students, Curious minds, Young entrepreneurs

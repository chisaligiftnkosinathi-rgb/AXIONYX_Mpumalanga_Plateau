# CAPABILITY TRACEABILITY #001
*The Nervous System of a Civilization*

## The Axiom
A defect is rarely a single bad part; it is a communication failure between nodes. Without traceability, blame moves randomly because nobody possesses the whole truth.

## The Equation
`System Integrity = Resources × Capability × Traceability × Learning`

## The Truth
Half-truths are fragmented realities. They destroy systemic integrity because they prevent learning. A society does not become advanced simply because it possesses raw materials. It becomes advanced because it maintains truthful, unbroken relationships (lineage) between every transformation of those materials. Traceability creates trust.

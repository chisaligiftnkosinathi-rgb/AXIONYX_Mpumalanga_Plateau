# AFRICAN INTELLIGENCE FEDERATION PROTOCOL
**Crystal ID:** AFRICAN_FEDERATION_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Continental Coordination & Interoperability
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Federation Law
> "A region becomes stronger when it can share its strengths while preserving its unique identity. Federation without absorption. Connection without loss of identity. Shared intelligence without centralised ownership."

## The Shared Capability Protocol (SCP)
Regions exchange capabilities, evidence, opportunity, and wisdom through a decentralized handshake. Mpumalanga's Analytical Chemistry can fuse with Gauteng's AI engineering without either region surrendering sovereignty.

## Wisdom Governance
All cross-border capability exchanges must pass the Melos Prime Continental Checks:
1. **Truth**: Is the capability evidence-based?
2. **Love**: Does it protect people?
3. **Order**: Does it create sustainable systems?
4. **Mercy**: Does it avoid extraction?

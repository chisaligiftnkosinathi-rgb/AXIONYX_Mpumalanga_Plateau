# CHAPPIES & NGIX: THE CURIOSITY ENGINE
**Crystal ID:** CURIOSITY_ENGINE_STANDARD_001
**Domain:** Seed Capability Generation
**Date Minted:** 2026-07-27

## Core Law
> "Industrial capability begins before employment. It begins when a mind encounters reality and chooses to understand it."
> "Every question is a potential capability seed. When curiosity is connected to evidence, learning, and opportunity, it becomes economic intelligence."

## The Origin Point
Before there is capability, there is curiosity. The Chappies Node models the earliest human behavior: asking "Why?" and "How?". It transforms raw curiosity into learning pathways.

## NGIX (Knowledge Intelligence Exchange)
NGIX maps what knowledge exists, where it connects, and what it can become. It acts as the bridge translating academic or foundational learning (e.g. studying Genetics) directly into practical capability (e.g. Biotechnology or Sampling Science).

## The Complete Capability Life-Cycle

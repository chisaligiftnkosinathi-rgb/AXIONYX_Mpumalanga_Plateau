# AXIONYX Translation Contract

However, the pipeline is strictly governed by the following rule:

> **Media may translate AXIONYX reality. Media may not create AXIONYX reality.**

The pipeline must strictly follow this unidirectional flow:

```text

        |
        v

        |
        v
Human Experience (Video, Presentation, Lesson)
```

```text

```

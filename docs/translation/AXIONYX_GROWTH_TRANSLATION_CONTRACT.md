# AXIONYX Growth Translation Contract

The AXIONYX Growth Engine strictly prohibits the generation of hallucinated marketing copy, unsupported claims, or unexplainable lead scoring. The translation of operational capabilities into business growth MUST adhere to the **Evidence First** doctrine.

## 1. What Claims Are Allowed
A marketing claim is only allowed if it is directly tied to a formalized Capability mapped in the AXIONYX Domain Model.
- **Allowed:** "Our laboratory provides coal quality testing using documented analytical methods." (Tied to the Capability graph)
- **Forbidden:** "We are the best coal laboratory in South Africa." (Subjective, unprovable)

## 2. What Evidence Is Required
Every generated asset (Company Profile, Advertisement, Education Material) must attach cryptographic or documentary proof of the capability:
- ISO Certifications
- Verified Method References
- De-identified Reality Event logs proving volume/experience

## 3. How a Capability Becomes Content
The `capability-translator` accepts an array of verified facts (e.g., equipment, personnel, historical throughput). It translates this strict context into a format tailored to the audience's problem. The AI renderer is constrained from adding adjectives that exaggerate the capability.

## 4. How Content Becomes an Opportunity
An opportunity is NOT a blind contact form. It is a sequence of recorded, deterministic events:
1. `customer_viewed_content`
2. `customer_understood_capability`
3. `customer_requested_information`
4. `qualified_opportunity_created`
This ensures the lead is fully context-aware before ever reaching human sales staff.

## 5. How Opportunities Remain Explainable
Lead scoring must not use black-box AI. It must output transparent, boolean logic:
```text
+ Customer requested coal testing (Match: Capability)
+ Location matches service area (Match: Geography)
+ Provider has evidence (Match: Trust)
Score: 82%
```
The reason for a lead score must always be explainable in plain text.

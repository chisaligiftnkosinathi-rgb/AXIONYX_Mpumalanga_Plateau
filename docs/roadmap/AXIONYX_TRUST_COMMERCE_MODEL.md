# AXIONYX Trust Commerce Model

**Objective:** Formalize the commercial architecture of AXIONYX by moving away from selling raw technology (AI, Knowledge Graphs) and instead selling the conversion of **reality into explainable intelligence**. 

This model draws inspiration from companies like Cartrack, which convert raw movement data (GPS) into actionable business outcomes (fleet optimization, risk reduction). AXIONYX will convert operational reality into Trust and Business Opportunities.

## 1. The Value Translation
Customers do not buy "Event Sourcing" or "Translation Engines." They buy outcomes.

| Raw Technology | Customer Meaning | Business Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Event Stream | See what happened | Reduce uncertainty |
| Knowledge Graph | Understand why it happened | Build trust |
| Evidence Layer | Explain decisions | Win customers |
| AI Translation | Discover opportunities | Improve decisions |

## 2. The AXIONYX Trust Experience Loop
We replace traditional marketing funnels with a continuous engagement loop built on evidence.

1. **Reality Captured:** Operational evidence is gathered (e.g., sample testing).
2. **Capability Connected:** The business's capability (e.g., laboratory analysis) is mapped to the evidence.
3. **Explanation Rendered:** The AXIONYX Content Factory translates this into an explainable narrative.
4. **Action Triggered:** The customer interacts with the narrative, converting trust into a qualified business opportunity.

## 3. Explainable Advertising
Traditional B2B advertising relies on unverified claims ("We are the best"). AXIONYX introduces **Explainable Advertising**:
- Campaigns are strictly tied to the `MarketingReality` schema.
- Content explains *why* a customer should trust the capability, backed by cryptographic evidence.
- The resulting lead is scored based on the customer's deterministic journey through the evidence layer.

## 4. Architectural Implementation: The Trust Experience
To facilitate this, the AXIONYX architecture will expand the `growth-engine` to include the `trust-experience` module.

```text
packages/
└── growth-engine/
    ├── capability-translator
    ├── audience-matcher
    └── trust-experience/ (NEW)
        ├── Landing Page Orchestrator
        ├── Proof Story Renderer
        ├── Evidence Explorer
        └── Lead Conversion Flow
```

The `trust-experience` ensures that every customer touchpoint is a journey from initial curiosity, through rigorous evidence exploration, ending in a highly qualified commercial lead.

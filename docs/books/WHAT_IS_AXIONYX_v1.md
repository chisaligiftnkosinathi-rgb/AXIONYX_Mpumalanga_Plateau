## Chapter 2 — What is Operational Intelligence?

Operational Intelligence means continuously observing reality and converting observations into decisions. It follows a simple, universal pipeline:

```text
Reality
   ↓
Observation
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Decision
   ↓
Knowledge
   ↓
Action
```

---

## Chapter 3 — What is AXIONYX?

> **AXIONYX is an Operational Intelligence Platform that connects to existing operational systems and transforms observations into explainable decisions, replayable evidence, and reusable knowledge.**

Crucially, AXIONYX complements existing systems rather than replacing them. It is the intelligence layer that sits above your operational reality.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Seven Stages

The platform's core lifecycle is deterministic and universal:

```text
Observe → Understand → Decide → Explain → Replay → Teach → Publish
```

* **Observe:** Capture telemetry from existing systems without disruption.
* **Understand:** Map raw data into an understandable operational ontology.
* **Decide:** Apply declarative policies to automate trusted decisions.
* **Explain:** Cryptographically link every decision to its origin evidence.
* **Replay:** Mathematically reconstruct any historical state.
* **Teach:** Synthesize intelligence into Academy lessons.
* **Publish:** Distribute knowledge automatically via Executive Reports and Media.

## Chapter 5 — The Evidence Chain

Every operational decision should instantly answer:
* What happened?
* Who observed it?
* Which policy applied?
* Why was the decision made?
* Can it be replayed?

This is the Evidence Chain. It establishes absolute traceability, transforming scattered data points into legally and operationally defensible truth.

## Appendix: Glossary of Terms

* **Observation:** Raw telemetry from a connected system.
* **Event:** An immutable record of something that occurred.
* **Evidence:** Cryptographically verifiable chains of events.
* **Policy:** Declarative rules governing automated decisions.
* **Workflow:** The sequence of steps required to complete an operation.
* **Digital Twin:** The real-time, queryable state of the operation.
* **Replay:** The capability to reconstruct past operational states identically.
* **Explainability:** The mathematical linkage between a decision and its evidence.
* **Operational Intelligence:** The conversion of observations into knowledge.
* **Connector:** System adapters mapping reality to the AXIONYX ontology.
* **Storyboard:** The canonical representation of an operational narrative.
* **Replay Package:** The portable artifact used to perfectly reconstruct a scenario.

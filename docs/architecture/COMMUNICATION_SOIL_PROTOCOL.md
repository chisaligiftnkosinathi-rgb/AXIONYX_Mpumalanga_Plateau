# COMMUNICATION SOIL PROTOCOL

## 1. The Principle of the Invisible Mycelium
"Do not force the seed to leave its soil. Bring the forest intelligence into the environment where the seed already exists."

The AXIONYX ecosystem observes reality where it naturally occurs. We do not require humans to download a new application to voice a problem. We connect directly to Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram) as our primary "Communication Soil."

## 2. Governance: Transport vs. Memory
**Meta is not the forest.** Meta is merely the climate where seeds are already growing. 
AXIONYX maintains strict sovereignty over:
- The Memory (Civilizational Knowledge)
- The Translation (ACRI)
- The Trust Graph
- The Capability Graph

Meta serves exclusively as the Transport Layer.

## 3. Data Sovereignty
Users own their capability identity. When a WhatsApp message enters the ecosystem:
1. It is logged as a raw `RealitySignal`.
2. It is immediately translated by ACRI into a generic capability requirement.
3. The original PII is decoupled from the `StructuredCapabilityNeed` when entered into the public Impande Genome.

## 4. Evidence Before Opportunity
Raw signals are merely "needs." A signal requires physical Evidence (photos, logs, data) before it becomes an actionable "Opportunity" that the African Capability Bank can fund. AI assists the translation, but it does not invent reality. If evidence is missing, the system marks the field as `UNKNOWN`.

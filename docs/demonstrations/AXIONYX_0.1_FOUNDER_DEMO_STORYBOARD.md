# AXIONYX 0.1 Founder Demo Storyboard

**Audience:** Laboratory executives, potential customers, partners, investors.
**Duration:** 3–5 minutes.
**Core Message:** "AXIONYX restores the missing connection between reality and decision."

---

**Visual:** A sprawling laboratory environment producing thousands of results.
**Narration:** "Every day, physical samples become decisions. But between the observation and the decision, the story often disappears."
**On Screen:**
```text
Sample → Result → Decision
[ MISSING: WHY? HOW? EVIDENCE? ]
```

**Visual:** Focus on a single dataset entering the system: `MP-2026-001`.



**Visual:** The Knowledge Graph illuminates, connecting the raw data to the wider operational ontology.


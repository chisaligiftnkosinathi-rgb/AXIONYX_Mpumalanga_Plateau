# AI Car Doctor Golden Path

## Scene 1: Vehicle Enters AXIONYX
- **Visual**: A Suzuki Ertiga on the road.
- **Narrative**: "A fleet vehicle is more than its GPS coordinate. It is an evolving story of health, maintenance, and reliability."

## Scene 2: Evidence Appears
- **Visual**: A cryptographic ledger appears, appending events: Registration, Maintenance at 120,000km.
- **Narrative**: "Instead of just tracking location, the AXIONYX Reality Layer records the vehicle's permanent evidence graph."

## Scene 3: Pattern Detected
- **Visual**: A real-time telemetry spike is detected against historical baselines.
- **Narrative**: "A temperature variation is observed. But raw data is not intelligence. It requires explanation."

## Scene 4: AI Explains
- **Visual**: The AI Car Doctor Interface loads. It does not just flash a red warning light. It displays the Decision Card.
- **Narrative**: "AXIONYX explains *why*. 'Temperature behaviour changed, pattern verified, maintenance interval analysed.' The system remembers how it knows."

## Scene 5: Recommendation
- **Visual**: The system connects the explanation to action.
- **Narrative**: "Because we understand the root cause deterministically, we can recommend precise action: Schedule a cooling system inspection."

## Scene 6: Service Opportunity (Growth Engine)
- **Visual**: The UI seamlessly routes the driver to the Cartrack Approved Workshop in Johannesburg North.
- **Narrative**: "And we close the loop. Trust is translated into a highly qualified service opportunity. From raw data to explainable action."

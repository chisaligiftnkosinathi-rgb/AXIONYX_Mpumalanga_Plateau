# AXIONYX Live Demonstration Guide
**Theme: "Operational Systems record work. AXIONYX explains, predicts, teaches, and publishes work."**

This is the master script for presenting AXIONYX to prospective customers. It is designed to sit *above* their existing investments, neutralizing the threat of a "rip-and-replace" migration.

## Chapter 1: The Acknowledgment (The Current State)
**Script:** "Your laboratory already operates successfully. You have a LIMS (System A) that handles sample registration, you have instruments running analyses, and you have analysts approving certificates. We are not here to replace that workflow. Operational systems are brilliant at *recording* work."

## Chapter 2: The Elevation (The Intelligence Gap)
**Script:** "But what happens after the certificate is produced? How do executives see cross-site trends? If an auditor asks *why* a specific instrument was paused, how long does it take to gather the evidence? AXIONYX is an Operational Intelligence Layer that sits on top of your existing LIMS and instruments."

## Chapter 3: Integration & Mission Control (The Digital Twin)
**Action:** Open the **Integration Explorer**.
**Script:** "Notice that AXIONYX connects to your LIMS, your Agilent ICP-MS, and even your SCADA systems simultaneously. It normalizes all of these into standard Observations."
**Action:** Switch to **Mission Control**.
**Script:** "This is your live Digital Twin. 742 active samples, a 99.4% Trust Score. We are constantly monitoring the evidence streaming from your existing systems."

## Chapter 4: The Executive Moment (Explainability)
**Action:** Click a failed Certificate in the dashboard and open the **Evidence Explorer**.
**Script:** "An anomaly was detected. Can your current system explain exactly why a workflow was halted? AXIONYX can."
**Action:** Trace the node downwards visually (Certificate → Workflow → Policy → Evidence → Observation).
**Script:** "Because AXIONYX evaluates policy against cryptographic evidence, we can trace this exact decision back to the exact ICP-MS output file and the analyst on duty, in real time."

## Chapter 5: The AI Demonstration (Content Orchestrator)
**Action:** Open the **AXIONYX Experience Studio**.
**Script:** "But evidence isn't just for auditors. It's for executives, marketing, and training."
**Action:** Select *Industry (Mining)* → *Systems (LIMS, SCADA)* → *Audience (CEO)* → *Output (Executive PDF, AI Video, Academy Lesson)*.
**Script:** "Within moments, the exact same immutable evidence chain used to satisfy the ISO auditor is transformed into an AI-generated training video, a PDF case study, and a LinkedIn post. That is true Operational Intelligence."

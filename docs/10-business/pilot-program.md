# AXIONYX Pilot Model

Organizations do not buy AI. They buy verified outcomes. 
The AXIONYX Pilot Program is not a software installation; it is a formalized scientific experiment designed to prove financial or environmental impact.

## The 7-Step Lifecycle

1. **Discovery**: Mapping the enterprise problem (e.g., "Crusher energy consumption is too high").
2. **Historical Baseline (Level 1)**: Ingesting 12 months of CSV data to estimate feasibility.
3. **Twin Creation**: Deploying the ATPS standard packages from the Twin Marketplace.
4. **Observation Period (Level 2 Baseline)**: Running the twin in passive mode for 14-30 days against live SCADA telemetry to ensure the model perfectly matches physical reality.
5. **Optimization Mission**: Releasing the AI Optimization Engine to formulate improvement proposals.
6. **Proof Engine**: The AI executes a command (via Human Governance approval). The Proof Engine monitors the results in reality.
7. **Impact Report**: The Value Assurance Engine generates the Verified Customer Outcome, detailing exact financial ROI and CO2 reductions.

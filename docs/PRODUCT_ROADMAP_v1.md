# AXIONYX Product Roadmap v1.0

## Status: ARCHITECTURE FROZEN

This document establishes the formal AXIONYX v1.0 baseline. The foundational architecture is strictly frozen. No new kernel capabilities will be introduced unless validated by real-world pilot feedback.

## The 7-Stage Pipeline
AXIONYX executes a universal pipeline across all industries:
**Observe → Understand → Decide → Explain → Replay → Teach → Publish**

## Strategic Horizons

### Horizon 1: The 90-Day Operational Sprint
Our singular focus is validating the locked architecture with real users across six parallel workstreams:
1. **Deploy the Public Demonstration Environment:** Launch `demo.axionyx.ai` as the live, interactive entry point for prospects and pilots.
2. **Build 10 Complete Replay Packages:** Construct polished scenarios across Laboratory, Mining, Water, Fleet, Manufacturing, Environmental, Food, and Healthcare domains.
3. **Complete the Python Content Factory:** Finalize the deterministic media pipeline (`Storyboard → OpenCV → MP4`) to output multi-modal assets.
4. **Connect to Real Operational Systems:** Expand live data integration via LIMS, OPC UA, MQTT, REST, and PLC adapters.
5. **Execute Pilot Engagements:** Transition from building software to validating measurable time saved with real organizations.
6. **Prepare for Production Operations:** Strengthen disaster recovery, automated backups, security scanning, and deployment rollback procedures.

### Horizon 2: The Digital Twin & Connector Marketplace
- **Connector Marketplace:** Expand the footprint by certifying new system adapters without altering the kernel.
- **Digital Twin Marketplace:** Provide reusable, domain-specific operational models (e.g., Laboratory Twin, Mining Plant Twin) pre-packaged with ontologies, policies, dashboards, and Academy lessons.

### Horizon 3: The Operational Intelligence Knowledge Graph
Interconnect domains, demonstrating that a solution in one industry (e.g., Fleet Telematics) automatically generates predictive intelligence for another (e.g., Supply Chain Manufacturing).

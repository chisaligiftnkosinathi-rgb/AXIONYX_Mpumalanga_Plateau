## Level 3: Operational Problem to Capability Mapping

### Example Pathway: Mining & Minerals Sector
1. **Operational Problem:** Rapid turnaround is required for ore grading; instrument downtime halts production.
2. **Required Capability:** Asset Intelligence & Turnaround Intelligence.
3. **Telemetry Sources:** XRF utilization metrics, LIMS sample queues.
4. **Standards & Policies:** ISO 17025, Internal Mining SLA Policies.
5. **Decision Workflows:** Automatic reallocation of urgent samples if primary XRF drifts or fails.
6. **Business Value:** Prevented production bottlenecks; guaranteed SLA compliance.

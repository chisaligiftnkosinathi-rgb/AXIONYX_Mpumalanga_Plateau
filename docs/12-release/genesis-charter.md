# AXIONYX Genesis Principles

> **"Why AXIONYX Exists"**
> Humanity has created increasingly powerful systems, but our ability to understand, trust, and govern those systems has not grown at the same speed. AXIONYX exists to create an evidence-driven intelligence layer between reality and action.

To protect this vision, all future development on AXIONYX must adhere to these five non-negotiable principles:

## 1. Evidence First
No intelligence without evidence. A model that cannot prove its provenance and trace its logic back to an observable data point is rejected by the kernel.

## 2. Reality Before Prediction
Models must remain connected to physical reality. If physical telemetry diverges from the model's simulation, the simulation is incorrect. Reality is the ultimate arbiter.

## 3. Human Governance
AI proposes. Humans authorize. The platform enforces hard cryptographic boundaries (Project Cerberus) preventing unauthorized AI execution on physical infrastructure.

## 4. Continuous Calibration
A model that stops learning becomes historical. The Digital Twin is a living artifact that must continuously ingest new data to maintain its ATRL certification.

## 5. Responsible Intelligence
Capability without governance creates risk. The Compliance Engine tests all intelligence proposals against environmental, legal, and safety boundaries before they ever reach a human operator.

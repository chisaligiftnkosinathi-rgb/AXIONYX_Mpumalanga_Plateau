# AXIONYX TRUST MODEL CONSTITUTION

> "Who is allowed to change reality inside AXIONYX?"

This constitution governs the Human Provenance layer of the `AXIONYX_PLATFORM`. Just as Sprint 2 established that every insight must trace back to its data origin, Sprint 3 establishes that every evidence submission and verification must trace back to its human origin.

## Rule 1: No Human Value Scoring
AXIONYX strictly forbids ranking humans like atoms. The system tracks `Roles`, `Capabilities`, `Contributions`, and `Signatures`. It does not calculate a generalized "Human Value Score." Identity is maintained for provenance, capability matching, and accountability.

## Rule 2: Multi-Domain Signatures for Maturity Advancement
A Reality Crystal cannot achieve high maturity through the approval of a single domain.
*   **C2 $\rightarrow$ C3**: Requires the signature of an academic/scientific Institution (e.g., University Lab).
*   **C3 $\rightarrow$ C4**: Requires cross-domain consensus: University + Industry + AXIONYX Guardian node.
*   **C4 $\rightarrow$ C5**: Requires Operational Evidence + Community Validation. No crystal may reach C5 without the signed consent of the community impacted by its implementation.

## Rule 3: Separation of Submission and Validation
An identity profile that submits an `evidence_submission` cannot be the same identity profile that provides the `verification_event` signature to validate it. Conflict of interest is cryptographically prevented.

## Rule 4: Institutional and Individual Provenance
A verified claim must hold the signature of both the individual `Researcher` and the `Institution` they represent, ensuring that systemic risk is borne by the responsible organizations, not just isolated individuals.

# AXIONYX Ethical AI Governance

AXIONYX rejects the "black box" AI paradigm for industrial infrastructure. AI systems controlling physical reality must be constrained by rigid, mathematically provable ethical boundaries.

## The Compliance Engine Boundary
AI models in AXIONYX do not possess executive authority. They propose optimizations.
Before any proposal is shown to a human operator, the **Compliance Engine** tests it against legal, environmental, and safety regulations.

If an AI suggests an efficiency gain that violates water conservation laws, the system issues a **Hard Block**. The optimization is deleted before human review.

## The Knowledge Firewall
When AXIONYX discovers a new optimization strategy via Federated Learning, the **Knowledge Governance Engine** prevents automatic data leakage. All intelligence is classified as:
- `INTERNAL_ONLY`
- `PARTNER_SHARED`
- `ANONYMIZED_GLOBAL`
- `PUBLIC_PRINCIPLE`

A human expert must explicitly authorize the elevation of knowledge into the `PUBLIC_PRINCIPLE` tier for the global Reality Exchange. 
Knowledge is shared. Raw enterprise data is strictly isolated.

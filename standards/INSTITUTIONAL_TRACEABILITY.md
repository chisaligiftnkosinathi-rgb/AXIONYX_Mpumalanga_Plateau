## Execution Ownership

This module is responsible for facilitating operational intelligence across the ecosystem. It provides the following capabilities to evaluate trust and ensure operational readiness:

- **Telemetry operational**: Aggregation of health and performance metrics.
- **Audit pipeline active**: Logging of critical operational actions.
- **Evidence capture active**: Collection of runtime evidence for certification authorities.
- **Deployment observable**: Exposing /.well-known/axionyx/manifest.
- **Health verified**: Health checks and liveness monitoring of ecosystem APIs.
- **Replay verified**: Capabilities for replaying operational traces for post-incident analysis.
